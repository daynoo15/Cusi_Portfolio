Business Recommendations

Digital First Strategy: Align marketing efforts with digital storefront cycles to capture maximum day-one revenue.

Hybrid Platform Focus: Invest in titles that leverage the portability of platforms like the Switch and Steam Deck.

Genre Diversification: Explore the growing "Indie" sub-sectors within RPG and Adventure genres which show high ROI.
